# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name
Ambient light for Facebook™

## Summary (max 132 characters)
Ambient glow behind photos and videos on Facebook, with a focus mode and detailed settings. Not affiliated with Meta.

## Category
Pick the closest one offered in the dashboard, e.g. **Make Chrome Yours → Functionality & UI** (or a Lifestyle/Entertainment category).

## Description
Make Facebook feel immersive: a soft, colorful glow spills out around every photo and video, picked from the media's own colors.

WHERE IT GLOWS
• News Feed: light spreads around posts, behind the post header and like bar
• Photo viewer and Reels: the whole dark background takes the media's colors
• Black and colored bars beside photos and videos turn into light
• Collages with a solid background: the background turns into light too (screenshots and text are left alone)

FOCUS MODE
• Blur and dim the left menu and right column so only the feed stands out
• Sharp again when you hover or type in search

FULLY ADJUSTABLE
• Blur, spread, opacity, brightness, contrast, saturation, fade-in
• Smooth motion for videos, debanding
• Turn each place on or off: photo viewer, Reels, feed photos, feed videos
• Quality and performance limits for slower computers
• Light and dark panel themes

LIGHTWEIGHT
• Lights media just before it scrolls into view, no flicker while scrolling
• Built-in performance overlay to check FPS on your own computer

PRIVATE
• No data collected, no tracking, no servers. Settings stay in your browser.

Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+A on/off, Alt+Shift+S settings. Or click the toolbar icon.
