# Privacy policy — Ambient light for Facebook™

_Last updated: 2026-10-04_

Ambient light for Facebook™ ("the extension") adds a soft glow behind photos and videos on facebook.com. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc.

## What the extension collects
**Nothing.** The extension does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data, browsing history, Facebook content, account information or analytics. It has no server and makes no requests to any server of its own.

## What happens on your device
- **Settings** you change in the extension's panel are saved with `chrome.storage.local`, in your browser only, so they persist between visits. Removing the extension deletes them.
- **Colors** for the glow are computed locally from the photos and videos already shown on the page. To read a photo's colors, the extension may load that same photo again from Facebook's own image servers (`*.fbcdn.net`); the result never leaves your device.
- The optional **Performance overlay** measures frame rate and timing in the current tab only and does not store or send these numbers anywhere.

## Permissions
- `storage` — to remember your settings.
- Access to `https://www.facebook.com/*` — to draw the glow on Facebook pages. The extension does not run on any other site.
